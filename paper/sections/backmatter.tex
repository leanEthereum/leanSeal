AI models were used extensively in this work, chiefly OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic's Claude Opus~5.5, under the authors' direction. The Rust and Python implementations, the benchmarks and the proof-of-concept contracts were written largely by these models from the specification. GPT-6 Astra audited the specification, the security argument and the earlier C implementation over nine rounds; the reports, and the changes made in response, are in the repository~\cite{leanSealRepo}. It also reviewed the proof of concept and this paper. GPT-6 Astra drafted the proof of \cref{thm:delay}, and Claude Opus~5.5 checked and revised it. Claude Opus~5.5 also helped prepare and edit the manuscript. The authors take full responsibility for the accuracy and correctness of the paper and for any issues in the implementation.
